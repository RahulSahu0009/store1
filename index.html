Master Optician Advice

Find Frames For Your Face Shape

Eyewear harmony is all about contrast and proportion. Select your face shape below to discover the silhouettes that naturally balance your features.

Oval Face Shape

Naturally Harmonious Proportions

An oval face features gentle curves with cheekbones slightly wider than the forehead and jawline. Because your facial proportions are naturally balanced, you can wear almost any frame geometry with confidence.

Recommended Silhouettes

  • Rectangular and square geometric frames (adds subtle structure)
  • Walnut & matte acetate classics that span the widest part of the face
  • Bold navigator sunglasses with strong upper brow bars

What to Keep in Mind

Avoid oversized frames that are wider than your cheekbones or sit excessively low, which can disrupt your face's natural symmetry.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know regarding our optical examinations, lens technologies, warranties, and boutique consultations.

Yes, absolutely. We welcome outside valid prescriptions from any ophthalmologist or hospital. Our optometrists will verify your pupillary distance (PD) and facial fitting measurements using our digital centering instrument before crafting your lenses. Alternatively, you may book our in-house 14-point examination for a fresh assessment.

Our 14-point optical protocol checks distance and reading acuity, digital computer screen eye strain, binocular coordination, astigmatic cylinder axis, corneal curvature, and non-mydriatic anterior ocular health. It takes approximately 30 minutes in our private testing suite.

We are certified dispense partners for Carl Zeiss Vision (Germany), Essilor (France), and Hoya (Japan). We offer single-vision, anti-fatigue occupational lenses, and personalized free-form progressive optics with DuraVision Platinum anti-reflective, BlueProtect, and photochromic Transitions® coatings.

Nearly all sunglasses in our collection can be custom-fitted with prescription lenses. We can match the original tint, introduce polarized filters, or apply flash mirror coatings so you get crisp 20/20 clarity without sacrificing sun aesthetics.

Standard single-vision lenses in stock index can be cut and hand-finished within 24 to 48 hours. Custom high-index progressive lenses or specialized German Zeiss surfacing typically require 4 to 6 business days for optical perfection and laser calibration.

Every frame purchased at Claré Optics includes a 1-year manufacturer warranty against craftsmanship defects. In addition, you enjoy lifetime complimentary ultrasonic deep cleaning, hinge lubrication, nose pad replacements, and ergonomic temple re-alignments whenever you drop by our Colaba studio.
